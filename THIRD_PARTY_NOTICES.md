## ats-scrapers

Provider URL-recognition and public ATS response-mapping behavior for the
SmartRecruiters and iCIMS adapters was informed by the `ats-scrapers` project.
Jobby reimplements that limited behavior inside its own bounded, no-redirect,
no-proxy source contracts; it does not include the upstream generic fetch,
proxy, browser, or TLS-impersonation layers.

Source: https://github.com/kalil0321/ats-scrapers
